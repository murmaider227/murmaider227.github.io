Glimmer Grove Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Glimmer Grove ("the app") is a puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data the app itself stores

Your level, coins, boosters and settings are saved only on your device. The app has no accounts and does not send this data to us.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped);
  • device and app information, and crash and performance diagnostics of the ad SDK.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Gameplay analytics

To see which levels are too hard and improve the game, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google LLC. It records gameplay events such as level started or finished, number of moves, time spent, boosters used and tutorial steps, together with an app instance identifier, device model, OS version, country and approximate location derived from the IP address. We do not collect your name, email or any other contact details. If you allow tracking, Google may also link this data with the Advertising Identifier for ad measurement.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS). The app asks for permission via App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and you see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. You are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Declining tracking or consent never blocks any part of the game.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
